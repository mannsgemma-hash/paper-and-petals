# Paper & Petals — Shop content reference

> An asset-author's checklist. Categories the SCR-06 Shop is built to hold, and
> the subcategories that should fill each one. Use this when commissioning,
> drawing, scanning, or briefing new packs.

The 12 categories below match the chip row on SCR-06 (`SHOP_CATEGORIES` in
`ui_kits/app/Shop.jsx`). The extra reference sections below (Seasonal,
Themes, Functional) are not separate chips today — they're packaging
strategies that re-mix items from the 12 categories into **Collections**.

## 12 · Collections

The strongest retention and revenue mechanic. A Collection bundles 24–32
hand-picked pieces drawn from the 11 categories above, around a single
emotional theme. Sell these — not individual stickers.

Each Collection ships with:

- 2–4 papers
- 6–10 stickers
- 4–8 ephemera
- 2–4 frames or containers
- 2–4 florals or details
- 1–2 tape / fastener
- 1–2 typography pieces

Initial Collection direction (priority order):

1. **Spring meadow** — wildflowers, soft botanical papers, hand-painted ribbon (cottagecore)
2. **Autumn library** — acorns, library cards, foxed pages, dried oak leaves (dark academia)
3. **Old romance** — love letters, cherry blossoms, lace handkerchiefs (romantic vintage)
4. **Coastal almanac** — tide charts, driftwood tones, seaside postage (coastal grandmother)
5. **Parisian café** — bistro menus, espresso stains, sketched pastries
6. **Antique botanist** — plant illustrations, herbarium tags, pressed specimens
7. **Moonlit garden** — celestial overlays, night-blooming florals, velvet tones (fairycore)
8. **Travel diary** — boarding passes, hand-drawn maps, hotel tags
9. **Witchy botanicals** — apothecary labels, dried herbs, tarot edges
10. **Winter cottagecore** — wool textures, snow-bleached papers, candle motifs

## Asset spec floor (for every pack)

Before any pack ships, it must have:

- Transparent PNGs at 2x retina (recommend ≥ 1024px on longest edge)
- A visible **paper-grain** layer or natural deckled edge — no flat vector look
- Soft drop shadow built into the asset OR rendered consistently by the editor
- Sized for the journal canvas — readable at editor zoom 0.6×–1.6×
- Named with the convention: `<category>-<pack-id>-<piece-index>.png`
  e.g. `floral-spring-meadow-04.png`
- Listed in the pack's `desc` (one line, ≤ 100 chars) and `items` count
- A new pack always launches as `isNew: true` for 14 days
